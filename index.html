Questions people ask before their first print.

What file formats can you print?

STL, 3MF or OBJ. Not sure what you have? Send it anyway. We'll tell you.

I don't have a 3D model. Where do I find one?

Any of the sites in the 60-second guide above. MakerWorld, Printables and Thingiverse have hundreds of thousands of free models. Find one you like and send us the page link.

Do I need a 3D printer or any software?

No. You only need WhatsApp. We handle slicing, printing, clean-up and shipping.

How much will my print cost?

15 Rs per gram of the finished print. We confirm the exact weight and price on WhatsApp before we start printing.

How long does a print take?

It depends on the size. Small things can be done fast, large ones take longer. We'll give you a realistic timeline when we see your model.

What colours can I choose?

PLA comes in lots of colours. Tell us your favourite and we'll let you know what's in stock.

How do I pay?

We share payment details on WhatsApp when you confirm your order.

Where do you deliver, and what are the delivery charges?

Anywhere in Pakistan by courier. We'll include the delivery charge for your city in your quote. (We only ship inside Pakistan for now.)

Can you print large or technical parts?

Usually, yes. Send the model and we'll check size, supports and strength, and tell you honestly if it will work.
