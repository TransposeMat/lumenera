A few things
you might wonder.

New to 3D printing? You don’t need to know the technical stuff. A model link is enough to start.

Ask us on WhatsApp
I don’t have a model. How do I find one?

Try Printables, MakerWorld or Thingiverse. You don’t need to download the file.

  1. Search for something you like.Try “vase”, “keychain” or “phone stand”.
  2. Open the model and look at its images.Pick the design you’d like us to check.
  3. Copy the page link and send it to us.We’ll check the licence and print suitability, then give you a quote.
Send the model you found
How much does a print cost?

Rs 15 per gram of the finished print. We confirm the model’s weight, print price and separate courier charge on WhatsApp before you approve. The pictured examples do not have fixed product prices.

Can I choose a colour or a different size?

Tell us what you have in mind. We’ll share available PLA colours and check whether your chosen size works for the model. Colours in example photos are inspiration, not a promise of stock or multi-colour printing.

Where do you deliver, and how long does it take?

We arrange courier delivery across Pakistan. Send your city and model so we can confirm the delivery charge and a realistic print and delivery timeline before you order.

How do I pay?

We confirm the payment method and details with you on WhatsApp when discussing your quote.

What files and materials can you print?

Send an STL, 3MF or OBJ file directly in WhatsApp, or just share a model page link. We currently print in PLA. For large, detailed or technical parts, send the intended size and use so we can check suitability first.

Are these photos of your own prints?

They’re examples from the model creators, including clearly labelled digital renders. They show the designs you can enquire about. Your finish and available colours are discussed in chat. See every source and credit.
