# Model and image credits

All twelve models and their creator photos are restored from the original Desktop Lumenera website. On 11 October 2026 the business owner confirmed special permission for their use, including the existing paid-printing offer and website imagery. Model and image permission are recorded separately as **user-confirmed** in `data/catalogue.json`; private grants were not independently inspected. The original published licence labels are retained as historical source metadata, not presented as evidence of a public commercial licence.

These are creator example photos, not photographs of Lumenera’s own prints. Accessories, plants and electronics are not included. Final size, weight, suitability and price are confirmed in chat. The original title may mention a designer’s weight; it is not a Lumenera quote.
